See how a class works

What you get in every class

  • Small groups

    Six students per teacher, so you get hands-on help at the wheel instead of watching from the back.

  • Everything included

    Clay, tools, aprons, firing and glazing are covered in your fee. Bring clothes you don't mind getting dirty.

  • Pieces you keep

    You finish four weeks with a bowl, a cup and a plate, fired and ready for the kitchen.

Four weeks, start to finish

  1. Week 1: Centre the clay. Wedge, centre and pull your first cylinder.
  2. Week 2: Shape. Turn cylinders into bowls and cups.
  3. Week 3: Trim and dry. Add feet, handles and your mark.
  4. Week 4: Glaze. Choose colours; we fire and you collect your pieces.
